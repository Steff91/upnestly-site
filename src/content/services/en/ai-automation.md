faq:
- q: Is our data used to train AI models?
  a: No. With the commercial APIs we use (Anthropic, OpenAI), your data is not used for training by default. For sensitive data we run open-source models on our own servers or yours, so nothing leaves that environment.
- q: Can you run AI without sending our data to OpenAI or Anthropic?
  a: Yes. We host open-source models on our own hardware or on your infrastructure. They are a good fit for document processing, internal search and assistants; for the hardest writing tasks the large cloud models are still ahead, and we tell you where the trade-off lies.
- q: What does a bigger AI project cost?
  a: Larger pipelines and agents run as a Custom project with a fixed quote after a short discovery. Once live, they are maintained through a monthly plan.
- q: Can we keep running the workflow without you?
  a: Yes. Workflows run in accounts in your name where possible, and every one is documented so your team or another developer can take it over.
