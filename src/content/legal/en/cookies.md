Cookies are small text files a website stores in your browser. Similar technologies, such as your browser's local storage, work the same way. This page explains what the Upnestly website uses and how you can change your choice.

## What we use today

**This website currently sets no tracking or advertising cookies.** Fonts and images load from our own server, without sending data to third parties.

We remember your cookie choice in your browser's local storage (key `upn-consent`), so we don't ask you on every visit. This record is necessary, stays in your browser and is never sent to us.

## How to change your choice

Use the **Cookie settings** link in the footer of any page to change or withdraw your consent at any time. You can also view, block or delete cookies in your browser settings — the website keeps working without them.

Send questions about cookies to [info@upnestly.net](mailto:info@upnestly.net). More about how we handle personal data is in our [privacy policy](/privacy/).
